# Nibbles Filter

Become your Nibble. A face-tracked filter that turns any of the 8,888 Nibbles into a live puppet for recorded clips and livestreams.

**Live:** https://geaux-eth.github.io/nibbles-filter/

- The Nibbles cast (Ghost, Wolf, Bear, Beaver, Gnome, Raccoon, Gecko, Cock): real drawn expression frames from the official model sheets, switched by your face (angry, talking, "oo", heavy-lidded, shocked)
- Holders' token Nibbles: the whole art moves with your head (tilt, sway, talk bounce)
- On-device face tracking (MediaPipe)
- Bonkd backdrops, Nibble color, green screen, or a custom upload
- Record 9:16, 1:1 or 16:9 clips with mic audio
- Stream mode for OBS (use Green screen + Chroma Key)
- Wallet gating via `ownerOf` on Ethereum mainnet (contract `0x5e52d41f0e40d7cdb204db0d09659846f7404547`)
