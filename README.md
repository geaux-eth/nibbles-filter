# Nibble FACE

Become your Nibble. A face-tracked filter for recorded clips and livestreams, in beta with four Nibbles: Ghost #4075, Bear #4248, Wolf #3133 and Gnome #8549.

**Live:** https://geaux-eth.github.io/nibbles-filter/

- On-device face tracking (MediaPipe); calibrates to your relaxed face, then reads small lip movements
- Drawn eyes and mouths for each Nibble, switched by your face with short cross-fades; blinks pass through half-closed
- Animated traits: Bear's orbiting stars, Wolf's spinning halo (wobbles with head movement), Gnome's dark magic
- Bonkd backdrops, Nibble color or green screen; record square, vertical or wide clips
- Stream mode: full-screen Nibble for OBS window capture
